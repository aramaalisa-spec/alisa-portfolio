Skyland Family is a membership-based investment platform for family offices and wealthy individuals.
While the industry best practice is to reduce friction on signup, any complex registration form that incudes reviewing every applicant comes with trade-offs. For a community founded on trust and deliberate integrity, carefully reviewing every prospective member is paramount.
